## Skill

- When it works: Needs a total of 15 or more Red-Eyes cards and/or Lord of the Red in your deck. This list has exactly 15, so do not replace a Red-Eyes card with a non-Red-Eyes card or the skill is off. Both effects can be used once per Duel.
- What it does: Effect 1: reveal Red-Eyes Black Dragon, Lord of the Red or Red-Eyes Transmigration from your hand, add Lord of the Red or Red-Eyes Transmigration from your deck, then add 1 Red-Eyes card. Effect 2: if you control a Red-Eyes monster (or the opponent controls a monster), add 1 of 5 monsters from outside your deck, send the others to the Graveyard, then add Polymerization.
