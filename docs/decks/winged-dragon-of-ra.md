## Skill

- When it works: Effect 1 is always on. Effect 2 can be used once per turn and needs a Ra in your hand to reveal. Effect 3 can be used once per Duel and needs a Divine-Beast on your side or a monster on the opponent's side (before 2026-04-09 it only worked if the opponent controlled a monster and you did not).
- What it does: Effect 1: during your turn you cannot Normal or Special Summon (except from the Extra Deck) any monster except Divine-Beast and Aqua monsters. Effect 2 (once per turn): reveal a Ra from your hand and play a Guardian Slime from your hand, deck or Graveyard (its effects are negated). Effect 3 (once per Duel): if you control a Divine-Beast, or your opponent controls a monster, set your LP to 4100 and add Monster Reborn from outside the deck to your hand; if you control no cards you can also add Ra or a card that mentions it from your deck.
