## Skill

- When it works: Effect 1 is always on. Effects 2 and 3 can each be used once per Duel. Effect 2 needs a Level 7 WATER Tuner in your hand to reveal (Ran Aegirine, Creation Aegirocassis); effect 3 needs a Level 10 Synchro Monster on the field or an opponent who controls a monster, so it works on both turn one (after your first Synchro) and turn two.
- What it does: Effect 1: any effect damage your cards would do becomes 0, except from Icejade Curse. Effect 2 (once per Duel): reveal a Level 7 WATER Tuner in your hand and send 1 card from your hand to the Graveyard, add up to 2 Icejade cards from your deck to your hand, then Set Icejade Cenote Enion Cradle from outside your deck. Effect 3 (once per Duel): if there is a Level 10 Synchro Monster on the field, or the opponent controls a monster, play 1 Icejade monster from your deck in face-down Defense Position (it cannot have the name of a monster you control), then Set 1 Icejade Spell/Trap from your hand or deck (you can activate it this turn).
