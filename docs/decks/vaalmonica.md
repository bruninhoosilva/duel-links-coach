## Skill

- When it works: Effect 1 is always on. Effects 2 and 3 can be used once per Duel each. Effect 2 needs a Vaalmonica monster in your hand to reveal (any of Angello, Dimonno or Selettrice). Effect 3 needs the opponent to control a card, so it is a turn-two effect (or when the opponent has a card on the field).
- What it does: Effect 1 (always): at the start of the Duel you get Pendulum Zones, and your opponent cannot target a Vaalmonica monster in your Pendulum Zone with card effects. Effect 2 (once per Duel): reveal a Vaalmonica monster in your hand and add 1 Vaalmonica card from your deck. Effect 3 (once per Duel): if your opponent controls a card, Set a Vaalmonica Spell/Trap from your hand or deck to your field (it can be activated this turn); if you Set it from your hand, you draw 2 cards and shuffle 1 card of your hand into the Deck.
