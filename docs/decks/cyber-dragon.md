## Skill

- When it works: Effects 2 and 3 can be used once per Duel; effect 1 (at the start of your first turn it sends a Cyber Dragon from the deck to the Graveyard and sets your effect damage to 0) is automatic. For effect 2 you need Cyber Dragon or Cyber Jormungardr in your hand, so both builds play Cyber Jormungardr ×3 and several Cyber Dragons.
- What it does: Effect 2: reveal Cyber Dragon or Cyber Jormungardr from your hand, add up to 2 Level 2 or lower Cyber Dragon monsters (Core, Nachster, Herz) and Set Cybernetic Overflow from outside the deck. Effect 3 (turn 2 onward): add up to 3 Cyber Dragon from your deck and/or Graveyard, but you cannot activate Spells other than Polymerization and Power Bond until you Special Summon Cyber End Dragon.
