## Skill

- When it works: Effect 1 happens once, at the start of the duel (it adds 2 Primathmech Alembertian and 3 Mathmech Circular to your deck). Effect 2 is the one you use every turn, but only once per turn.
- What it does: A Level 4 Mathmech Tuner in your hand (Mathmech Sigma, Diameter or Nabla), or Cynet Mining to fetch one. Effect 2: reveal that Tuner, send 1 card from your hand or field to the Graveyard, then add 2 Mathmech monsters from your deck to your hand. From turn 2 on you can also set 1 Mathmech Spell/Trap from your hand or deck.
