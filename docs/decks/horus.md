## Skill

- When it works: Needs 6 or more DARK monsters in your deck: Imsety x3 and D.D. Crow x3 are exactly 6, so do not swap a D.D. Crow for a card that is not DARK. You can use it once per turn and twice per duel.
- What it does: No special card is needed. During your Main Phase put 1 card from your hand on the bottom of your deck or send it to the Graveyard, then draw 2 cards (3 if it is the first turn of the player going second). The deck has no way to search, so the skill is what gives it consistency.
