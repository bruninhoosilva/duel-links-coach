## Skill

- When it works: Effects 2 and 3 can be used once per Duel. The first effect only turns your own effect damage to 0 (except for cards that mention Shining Sarcophagus). To use effect 2 you need to reveal a Spellcaster that mentions Shining Sarcophagus, so keep several in the deck: Silent Magician Zero ×3, Dark Magician Girl ×2 and Dark Magician.
- What it does: Effect 2: reveal 1 Spellcaster monster that mentions Shining Sarcophagus from your hand, then add up to 2 cards from your deck, choosing from Shining Sarcophagus and monsters that mention it. If only your opponent controls a card, you can also play Silent Swordsman Zero from your hand or deck. Effect 3 (turn 2 onward): send Silent Swordsman Zero from your field to the Graveyard, play Silent Swordsman LV7 face-down in Defense Position from outside your deck, and add Silent Sword Slash if you control the Sarcophagus.
