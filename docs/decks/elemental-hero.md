## Skill

- When it works: Effects 2 and 3 can be used once per Duel. To use effect 2 you need Elemental HERO Spirit of Neos or Miracle Ejector in your hand, so the deck plays 3 of each and Stratos to find a HERO when you did not draw them. Effect 3 needs you to control Spirit of Neos or the opponent to control a monster.
- What it does: Effect 2: reveal Spirit of Neos or Miracle Ejector, add an Elemental HERO monster from your deck and Polymerization from outside your deck. Effect 3: Set Super Polymerization and E - Emergency Call from your deck. Effect 1 adds 12 Fusion Monsters to your Extra Deck, limits your Special Summons to Neos, Elemental HERO and Neo-Spacian monsters, and lets you activate Super Polymerization and Favorite Contact the turn they are Set.
