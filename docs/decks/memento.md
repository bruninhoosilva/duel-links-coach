## Skill

- When it works: Needs 6 or more DARK monsters in your deck, which every Memento list has. You can use it once per turn and twice per duel.
- What it does: No special card is needed. During your Main Phase put 1 card from your hand on the bottom of your deck or send it to the Graveyard, then draw 2 cards (3 if it is the first turn of the player going second). Sending a card to the Graveyard is good in this deck, and the creator says Mementotlan Ghattic is the best card to put back.
