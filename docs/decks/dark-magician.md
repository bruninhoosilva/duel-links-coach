## Skill

- When it works: Effect 1 happens once at the start of the duel (nothing for you to do). Effects 2 and 3 can each be used once per duel, and only if your deck holds 15 or more Dark Magician cards and cards that mention it.
- What it does: Effect 2 needs Timaeus the United Dragon in your hand: shuffle 1 other card from your hand into your deck, Timaeus is played from your hand, and you add 2 Dark Magician cards. Illusion of Chaos and Preparation of Rites (which searches Illusion of Chaos) are ways to get Timaeus. Effect 3 needs you to control "Dark Magician" or your opponent to control a monster: it plays Dark Magician or Dark Magician Girl from your deck face-down and gives you The Eye of Timaeus.
