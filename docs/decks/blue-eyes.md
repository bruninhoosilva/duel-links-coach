## Skill

- When it works: Both effects can be used once per Duel and need a deck that holds 15 or more Blue-Eyes White Dragon cards or cards that mention it (this list has about 16; I counted from the card texts, so check the number if you change the list).
- What it does: Effect 1: reveal a Blue-Eyes monster from your hand and add Blue-Eyes White Dragon or another card that mentions it from your deck (usually Kaibaman the Legend), then Blue-Eyes Ultimate Dragon and Black Luster Soldier go to your Graveyard from outside your deck. Effect 2: if you control a Blue-Eyes monster, or only your opponent controls a monster, Set a Spell/Trap that mentions Blue-Eyes White Dragon (you can activate it the same turn).
