## Skill

- When it works: Effect 2 can be used once per turn and twice per Duel. Effect 1 is always on: you can only Summon Swordsoul, Ecclesia, Tri-Brigade, Springans and Icejade monsters and Fallen of Albaz or cards that mention it (Extra Deck Special Summons are not restricted).
- What it does: Effect 1: you cannot Normal or Special Summon any monster (except from the Extra Deck) that is not a card that mentions Fallen of Albaz, Fallen of Albaz itself, or an Ecclesia, Tri-Brigade, Springans, Swordsoul or Icejade monster. If the opponent has a card on the field or in the Graveyard you can Normal Summon an extra monster in your Main Phase. Effect 2 (once per turn, twice per Duel): reveal Fallen of Albaz or a monster that mentions it, then add 2 cards from Ecclesia, Tri-Brigade, Springans, Swordsoul, Icejade cards and/or Branded in High Spirits from your deck to your hand.
